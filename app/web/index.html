Methodological Categorisation

Measured Findings
  • Baseline Floor: Frozen ordinal regression yields 2.16 g/dL MAE and $R^2 \approx 0.013$ on 43 held-out patients.
  • Illumination Confound: Italian cohort is uniformly ~20% brighter in ambient luma than Indian cohort, shifting cross-site transfer error to 4.36 g/dL.
  • Abstention Gate: Abstaining on lowest-confidence quartile did not reduce MAE (2.16 g/dL at 100% coverage rose to 2.26 g/dL at 25% coverage). Ships strictly as a quality gate.
  • Severe Anaemia Deficit: The benchmark corpus holds exactly zero severe anaemia cases (<7.0 g/dL) across all 217 patients.
Research Hypotheses
  • Dermal Melanin Bypass: Palpebral conjunctiva has minimal melanocyte density; targeting this ROI can decouple haemoglobin absorbance from epidermal pigmentation.
  • Ordinal Soft Loss: Continuous expectation over ordered Gaussian soft bins preserves calibration better than unconstrained MSE regression.
  • Illumination Normalisation: Grey-world white balancing and illuminant-invariant colour ratios ($a^*/b^*$) mitigate exposure discrepancies across device sensors.
Future Scope
  • Multi-Spectral Hardware: Dedicated narrow-band 540 nm / 577 nm LED illumination to isolate haemoglobin Q-bands cleanly from ambient light.
  • Prospective Clinical Cohort: Multi-center IRB/IEC trial with venous Coulter Counter reference standard and ≥25% Fitzpatrick V–VI representation.
  • Self-Supervised Pretraining: Ophthalmic foundation models to learn conjunctival microvascular morphology before head fine-tuning.

Core claims (C1–C4)

C1: Continuous vs Categorical

Evaluated

Does continuous ordinal Hb regression outperform binarised classification? (Measured baseline: Ordinal MAE 2.16 vs Binary 2.14 g/dL)

C2: Targeted Conjunctiva Masking

Evaluated

Does segmenting palpebral conjunctiva outperform whole-frame measurement? (Segmented features consistently reduce variance across lighting shifts)

C3: Pigmentation Fairness (ITA°)

Audited

Is error correlated with skin tone (Individual Typology Angle)? Audited using OLS gate; flag set at |m| ≥ 0.002 g/dL/°ITA.

C4: Multi-Site Generalisation

Confound Disclosed

Cross-site transfer between India and Italy reveals a ~20% ambient illumination confound, shifting cross-site error to 3.3–4.4 g/dL.

Selective prediction: value of abstaining

Refusing to estimate when the photograph is poor is a safety behaviour, not an accuracy claim. In the first frozen run, rejecting the lowest-confidence images did not reduce mean absolute error (ordinal MAE 2.16 g/dL at full coverage rose to 2.26 at 25% coverage), so the gate is shipped for safety only.

Corpus limitation: zero severe cases

Across all 217 patients (126 normal, 76 mild, 15 moderate), there are ZERO severe anaemia cases (<7.0 g/dL). This tool cannot be validated on severe anaemia until a prospective cohort is enrolled.

Methodology, Optical Physics & Limitations

How Haeraombin works

The camera captures the palpebral conjunctiva (inner lower eyelid lining), which provides direct optical access to microvascular blood beds with negligible melanin attenuation. Local computer vision extracts calibrated colour coordinates (CIELAB, Erythema Index, a*/b*) and feeds a normalized tensor into an ONNX MobileNetV3 ordinal regression model via browser WebAssembly. Zero pixels ever leave the device.

Light absorption & skin pigmentation

Why smartphone screening is difficult: Haemoglobin and melanin absorb light in overlapping spectral wavelengths (490–577 nm).

ITA: +25° (Intermediate)

Notice how melanin absorbance extends across the blue-green spectrum where haemoglobin Q-bands sit. Targeting the palpebral conjunctiva (which has negligible melanin) avoids this dermal confounding.

What Haeraombin cannot do

This is an experimental screening aid, not a clinical diagnostic instrument. It cannot replace venous laboratory haematology (Coulter counter/Sysmex), cannot diagnose anaemia, and cannot rule it out. An abnormal estimate is merely a prompt for professional medical follow-up.

Do not use in medical emergencies or for therapeutic dosing decisions.

Fairness and responsible AI

Performance is audited across skin pigmentation (Individual Typology Angle - ITA°) and imaging cohorts. Where significant error disparities exist, they are explicitly flagged rather than concealed behind aggregate metrics.

Clinical validation protocol (Planned)

Prospective protocol planned with secondary healthcare centers: venous CBC reference, ethical clearance (IEC), minimum 25% deep pigmentation cohort.

  • Reference Standard: Automated Coulter counter / Sysmex venous complete blood count within 30 minutes of photograph.
  • Ethics: Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) clearance with written informed consent.
  • Subgroup Target: Enrolling ≥25% individuals with ITA° < 10° (Fitzpatrick V–VI) to ensure statistical power for C3 gate.
  • Clinician Advisory: Collaborative protocol review with registered haematologists.

Anonymous telemetry & privacy guarantee

No photographs, biometric features, or patient identifiers are ever sent over the network. Only inference latency (ms) and general WHO classification counts are collected for runtime reliability.

Zero cloud transmission: All image processing and deep learning inference occur purely inside the local browser sandbox via WebAssembly.
